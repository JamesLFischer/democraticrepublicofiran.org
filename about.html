ABOUT

Independent coverage focused on Iran and its global community.

DemocraticRepublicOfIran.org is an independent, unaffiliated Iran-focused publication and news discovery site. It is not an official government, party, campaign, or opposition website.

What the site does

It follows recent reporting covering Iran, democracy, civil society, human rights, the economy, culture, and the Iranian diaspora. Headlines are source-attributed, and readers can open the original publisher for the complete article.

Editorial approach

The visual identity is deliberately Iran-centered, while political reporting remains source-attributed. The aggregator does not declare a preferred party, candidate, government, opposition movement, or political outcome. Stories are primarily ordered by publication time after automated duplicate removal.

Why an aggregator

Coverage of Iran is spread across international outlets, Iranian media, academic institutions, diaspora publications, and specialist sources. A single feed makes it easier to see what different publishers are reporting without republishing their full work.

Independence

This site should remain clearly labeled as independent and unaffiliated unless its ownership or organizational status changes. Add a real contact address and ownership disclosure here before a public launch.
