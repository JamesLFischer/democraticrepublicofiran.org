NewsSources & Method

ABOUT

A news and media project focused on Iran and its global community.

DemocraticRepublicOfIran.org presents current reporting about Iran alongside material from the project's collaborators.

Democratic Republic of Iran is a collaboration for a free Iran between:

  • The Trump Administration
  • Iran National Council (INC)
  • National Union for Democracy in Iran (NUFDI)
  • Iran Prosperity Project
  • Reza Pahlavi

What the site does

It follows recent reporting covering Iran, democracy, civil society, human rights, the economy, culture, and the Iranian diaspora. Headlines are source-attributed, and readers can open the original publisher for the complete article.

Editorial approach

News coverage remains source-attributed. Stories are primarily ordered by publication time after automated duplicate removal, and readers are directed to the original publisher for the full article.

Why this news feed

Coverage of Iran is spread across international outlets, Iranian media, academic institutions, diaspora publications, and specialist sources. A single feed makes it easier to see what different publishers are reporting without republishing their full work.

Project

The Democratic Republic of Iran is the brain child of Phil Anderson and Reza Pahlavi.
